## 2026-10-26 - Floating Widget Trigger & Modal Accessibility Pattern
**Learning:** Animated floating widgets (like AI chatbots or Lottie badges) are frequently implemented as clickable `<div>` wrappers without keyboard accessibility or ARIA context. Moving the trigger to a semantic `<button type="button">` with `aria-label` and `aria-expanded`, and wrapping the inner Lottie animation in a `<span>` with `pointerEvents: 'none'`, creates a clean keyboard-accessible control. Additionally, adding an explicit header close button (`&times;`) and `aria-live="polite"` on message logs lets screen reader users manage floating widgets seamlessly.
**Action:** Always wrap animated Lottie toggles in semantic `<button type="button">` elements with explicit `aria-label` and `pointerEvents: 'none'` on the Lottie wrapper.
