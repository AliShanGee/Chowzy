## 2025-05-18 - Transform 404 Dead Ends into Accessible Navigation
**Learning:** Replacing accusatory error messages ("you typed a wrong URL") on 404 pages with friendly microcopy, a semantic `<main>` landmark, and explicit ARIA labels on decorative icons (`role="img"`) and CTA buttons turns confusing abrupt dead ends into welcoming, accessible user navigation paths.
**Action:** Always wrap error/404 screens in `<main>` landmarks, include accessible decorative graphics with proper ARIA attributes, and provide clear, reassuring CTA links back to primary content.
