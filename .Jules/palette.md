## 2026-08-04 - Descriptive Navigation ARIA Labels
**Learning:** Back and navigation buttons with symbol/arrow glyphs (e.g., `&larr;` or standard icons) are often misidentified or hard to understand for screen reader users if they don't have explicit labels. Adding descriptive, state- or context-dependent `aria-label` tags ensures accessibility guidelines are fully met.
**Action:** Always verify that icon/symbol-only action elements (especially buttons containing raw arrow symbols or back links) have clear, accessible descriptions and/or `aria-label` attributes.
