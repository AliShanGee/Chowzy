## 2026-07-08 - Enhanced Empty Cart State & Accessibility Refactor
**Learning:** Empty states are high-value real estate for UX delight; replacing plain text with a Lottie animation and a clear "Browse Foods" CTA significantly improves the user's journey. Additionally, interactive 'back' arrows implemented as divs are common accessibility blockers.
**Action:** Always provide a clear call-to-action in empty states. Consistently refactor non-semantic interactive divs to <button type="button"> with aria-labels to ensure keyboard and screen reader accessibility.
